DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Bold -- bundled here (2026-08-02) because reportlab's
built-in Helvetica/Helvetica-Bold have no Cyrillic glyphs at all, which made
every Russian label on the per-unit PDF export render as solid black boxes.
DejaVu is a Bitstream Vera derivative with full Cyrillic coverage, freely
redistributable. See app/services/unit_pdf_service.py for where it's loaded.
